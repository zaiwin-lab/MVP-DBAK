## 2. Fact-check flags
1. 1922 declaration date conflict: index says 22/11/1922, sejarah-rb says 10/11/1922 → present as "November 1922" pending document check.
2. Construction year: 1863 per family account; some commentary suggests ~1890 concrete period → present both honestly (done on old site too).
3. "Largest/first" claims ("rumah terbesar…", "second largest after the Astana") → attribute as family/historical accounts, not independently verified.
4. 1,500 descendants = 2020 estimate for generations 3–7 only → label as historical estimate.
5. 282+ burial plots = as of March 2021 → date-stamp.
6. 19 Apr 2025 Ramah Tamah: named on old-site homepage banner; photo public on their Facebook → usable; individual names/titles NOT published without approval.
7. Current committee (2024/2026 session names) — NOT verifiable from available sources → placeholder with term dates, listed in CONTENT-REVIEW.md.
