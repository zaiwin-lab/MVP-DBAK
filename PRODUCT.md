# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Descendants of Datu Bandar Abang Haji Muhammad Kassim (Waris 8 DBAK) across
Malaysia and abroad, from elders who remember Rumah Batu to grandchildren who
have never seen it; plus researchers, partners, and Sarawak heritage
institutions. Visitors arrive on phones from WhatsApp family groups more often
than on desktops. The job to be done: understand the house, the family, and the
restoration journey within minutes, feel proud and included, and find a
concrete way to participate.

## Product Purpose

A public heritage home for Rumah Batu (built 1863, Kuching) and the eight
family lines of DBAK. It preserves verified history, shows the restoration
journey honestly (no overclaiming), celebrates gatherings and work since 2020,
and invites younger generations into stewardship. Success: a first-time visitor
understands house + family + journey in a few minutes and knows how to help.

## Brand Personality

Dignified, rooted, generous. Warm and factual; proud without triumphalism;
calm and forward-looking. The emotional core: "Eight family lines. One shared
heritage. A living legacy carried forward together." Bahasa Melayu is the
primary voice, polished and human, never bureaucratic.

## Anti-references

- Generic NGO/charity templates and guilt-based fundraising blocks.
- Generic Islamic stock ornaments, excessive gold, glossy gradients, floating
  glass cards, auto-rotating carousels, animation for its own sake.
- The old Weebly site's flat hierarchy and screenshot-heavy galleries.
- Corporate "masterplan" render aesthetics that overpromise restoration.

## Design Principles

1. The photograph is the design: real family and house imagery leads; chrome
   recedes.
2. Honesty is a visual feature: status labels, source notes, and dated captions
   are designed elements, not fine print.
3. Eight-as-one: the eight-branch motif recurs quietly (structure, not
   ornament).
4. Elders first: readable sizes, generous contrast, obvious navigation; works
   on an old phone over slow data.
5. Nothing private: research inputs stay research inputs; the public layer is
   privacy-safe by construction.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Keyboard navigable, visible focus, skip link, semantic HTML,
alt text on every image, ≥4.5:1 body contrast, prefers-reduced-motion honored,
fully usable without JavaScript, responsive from 320px. Bilingual BM (primary)
and EN with per-page language switch.
